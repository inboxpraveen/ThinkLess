# Code of conduct

ThinkLess is built by people with different backgrounds, levels of
experience and opinions. We want taking part to be a good experience for all
of them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including in disagreement.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Give credit, and accept feedback on your own work graciously.
- Help newcomers find their way, and be patient with questions.
- Keep discussions focused on what is best for the project and its users.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other channel where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/inboxpraveen). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, and temporarily or
permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful, with an explanation
where appropriate.
